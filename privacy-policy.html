Privacy Policy

Effective date / Last updated: July 24, 2026

SacMusicals ("SacMusicals," "we," "us," or "our") provides tabla making, reheading, and repair services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through sacmusicals.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you enter: first name, last name, email address, phone number (optional), and your message. This information is submitted directly to our form processor, Web3Forms, which delivers it to us by email. We use it solely to respond to your inquiry.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our Site — for example, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and what device or browser they're using. Google Analytics collects this information through cookies and similar technologies, and may see your IP address (used to approximate general location, such as city or region, not a precise address).

We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with the information you submit through our contact form.

2. Cookies

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. We use cookies placed by Google Analytics to measure Site traffic and usage. Specifically:

  • _ga / _ga_* — used by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and sessions. These typically expire after up to 2 years unless cleared sooner.

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies, and we do not sell or share cookie data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Your choice: When you first visit our Site, you'll see a short notice about analytics cookies. You can decline them at any time, and you can change your mind later using the Cookie Preferences link in the footer of every page. Declining stops future analytics data collection on this Site and removes existing Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

You can also control cookies directly through your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Note that our Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, as no common industry standard for interpreting them exists yet.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To understand how visitors use our Site, so we can improve it
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of our Site

We do not sell your personal information, and we never will.

4. Third Parties We Work With

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — website usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact form submissions to us. See Web3Forms' Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry. Google Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics account settings, which we've configured to align with standard practice for a small business site.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Regardless of where you live, you can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete that information
  • Ask us to stop using your information for a particular purpose

Email us at sactablamaker@gmail.com and we'll respond promptly.

California Privacy Disclosures

SacMusicals may not currently meet the revenue or data-volume thresholds that trigger the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We're providing the following disclosures voluntarily, and we honor requests from California residents the same way we would if the law required it:

  • We collect the categories of information described in Section 1 above.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under the CCPA.
  • You may request access to, deletion of, or correction of your personal information by emailing us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent changes.
